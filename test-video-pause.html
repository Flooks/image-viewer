This test verifies that videos are paused when navigating to a different content source (Requirement 10.5)

Test Setup

This test will:

  1. Create a StateManager and MediaGallery
  2. Render video posts in the gallery
  3. Simulate video playback
  4. Navigate to a different content source
  5. Verify that all videos are paused
