🔐 Reddit Authorization

Step 1: Authorize

Click the button below to open Reddit's authorization page:

Open Reddit Authorization →

Step 2: Allow Access

On the Reddit page, log in if needed, then click "Allow".

After clicking Allow, Reddit will try to redirect to a redreader:// URL. Your browser will show an error or just stay on the page — this is expected!

Step 3: Copy the Redirect URL

After clicking Allow, look at your browser's address bar. It should now show something like:

redreader://rr_oauth_redir#access_token=abc123...

If your browser shows an error page or "can't open this page", the URL is still in the address bar — copy it!
